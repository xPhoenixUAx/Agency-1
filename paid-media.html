Decision framework

Investment follows evidence, not platform activity.

Clicks and platform conversions provide useful signals, but commercial value determines what deserves more budget.

Start with economics

Targets reflect margins, sales capacity, customer value and the real threshold for sustainable acquisition.

Protect signal quality

Conversion actions are reviewed so bidding learns from meaningful demand instead of inflated activity.

Scale in controlled steps

Budgets expand where performance is repeatable, measurement is reliable and operational capacity can support growth.
